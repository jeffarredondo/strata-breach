# Rules, paper trading from Mon 2026-10-19 (updated 2026-10-18)

**Symbols / timeframes:** IAU, PPLT, IBIT (spot BTC ETF; was BTCUSD until 2026-10-24), all DAILY bars (changed from IAU/PPLT 4H on 2026-10-19, see Changes). Long only, no leverage.
**Pots:** two $5k paper pots, tracked by the `Pot` column in `log/trades.csv`: `you` (visual, discretionary) and `script` (whatever `scan.py` says, taken mechanically). $1,667 per trade (33% of the pot, so three open positions use the whole pot and never need leverage; was $1,000 / 20% on 10/19), max 3 open (one per symbol). Both pots start flat on 10/19; ignore the IAU "model position" that already existed on 10/16.

1. **Lines:** falling lines anchor on wicks, rising lines on candle bodies. Top-down from monthly to the trading timeframe. Marked at the end of a session, never during a trade.
2. **Entry:** a full bar closes above a falling line with >=3 touches; buy at the NEXT bar's open (log the fill versus that open). If the signal is missed, wait for the next one. No late entries (log as "late, not a signal" if taken anyway).
3. **Stop:** entry - 1.5 x ATR(14), stop-market GTC, set after the fill (placeholder until then). It is the loss limit, not the exit plan. The stop only fires in market hours on IBIT; a gap through it fills at the open.
4. **Exit (script pot):** swing-low trail. Swing low = low below the 3 bars either side, confirmed 3 bars later. Trail = highest confirmed swing low since entry (floor: the 1.5 ATR stop). A bar closes at or below the trail -> sell at the next open. No max hold; the scan flags a trade open more than 30 bars so you can decide by hand whether to keep it.
5. **Exit (your pot):** your read of structure (HH/HL, LL/LH, lines). Log why when you differ from the script.
6. **Chart:** falling lines, 9/21, stop and trail levels, LL/LH levels. Supertrend removed. 9/21 fresh cross (EMA9 above EMA21 within the last 5 bars) is a log field only, not a gate.
7. **Log (`log/trades.csv`):** Pot, Symbol, TF, LineType, EntryTime, EntryPx, StopPx, Fresh921, Pokes10, ExitTime, ExitPx, ExitReason, RetPct, Label (up/down/chop), Notes. A signal you skip gets a row with ExitReason=SKIPPED and the reason in Notes. When your exit differs from the script's, put the script's exit date/price in Notes.
8. **Routine:** run `python3 scan.py` (or the GitHub Action) once a day: after the 16:00 ET close, weekdays only (IAU, PPLT, IBIT all close at 16:00 ET; nothing to do on weekends or market holidays). The Action appends the output to `log/scan_log.md` whenever it changes, which is the record of what the script said at the time. `--wicks` prints the touch points of a line; `--positions log/trades.csv` adds the readout for your own open trades.
9. **No parameter changes in the first sample.** If one changes, add a dated line below.

## Changes
- 2026-10-18: added PPLT and BTC; two pots; 1.5 ATR stop replaces the 1% stop (tighter than the tested floor on IAU 4H); swing-low trail is the script exit; scanner added.
- 2026-10-19: all three symbols on DAILY bars. 4H lost to daily on every symbol over 2024-11..2026-10 (swing-low trail, CAGR @100%: IAU +8.7 vs +21.9, PPLT -9.4 vs +26.0, BTC -10.4 vs +11.8). The one open discretionary BTC 4H trade (entered 10/19 ~05:30 ET) plays out by the swing-low rule and is the only 4H exception.

- 2026-10-20: stake per trade 20% -> 33% ($1,667). Sizing only, no signal or exit change; `RetPct` is still the metric of record. Backtest 2015-2026 (BTC+PPLT, intraday 1.5 ATR stop + swing trail): pot +24% / maxDD -23% at 33% vs +14% / -15% at 20%. 50% was rejected: three open would be 150% of the pot.
- 2026-10-20: stop is a stop-market at fill - 1.5 ATR (fires intraday), the swing-low trail is the exit; a missed stop order costs about half a point a year in the backtest. Daily bars only. A close within 0.1 ATR of the line is a touch, not a break (marginal-break test, `research/FINDINGS.md`).
- 2026-10-22: removed the 120-bar max hold. It never fired on BTC or PPLT in the backtest (identical results with and without); on IAU 2005-18 it clipped one trade (CAGR @33% +0.6% vs +0.5% without). Trades open more than 30 bars are flagged in the scan for a manual keep-or-end decision; it is not a rule.
- 2026-10-22: the scan now prints the failed-poke count (bars in the last 10 / 20 whose high went above the falling line but closed back under it) on every entry signal and on the model position's signal line. Log field only (`Pokes10`), never a gate: on IAU 2015-26 signals with >=1 poke in 10 bars averaged -0.2% vs +2.4% without (one-sided p~0.03), PPLT null, BTC same direction but not significant.
- 2026-10-22: the scan does not check the intraday stop (tried, reverted). The stop in item 3 is something you watch: if price touches it, exit. The scan readout is close-based (trail and floor) only.
- 2026-10-24: BTC vehicle changes from BTC-USD (24/7 bars) to IBIT (weekday session bars) for the script pot from Mon 10/26; same rules, same 33% stake. Reason: the same strategy on IBIT-hours bars rebuilt from Bitstamp hourly (validated against real IBIT, 2024-26: close-to-close corr 0.999, gap distribution matches) returned +16.1% / maxDD -17.4% at 33% (2018-09..2026-10, 4 bps) vs +14.6% / -18.4% on 24/7 bars and +11.7% / -21.3% at 150 bps (Schwab spot at 75 bps a side). A market-hours stop does as well as a 24h stop. The one discretionary BTC-USD trade (fill 82,805, stop 79,648.86) was stopped out on 10/24 at the stop, -3.8%.

## What is and isn't tested
- All backtests are on DAILY bars (IAU/PPLT 2015-2026, BTC 2015-2026), which is what the scanner now runs. 4H was tested on 2024-11..2026-10 (the ~2 years Yahoo keeps) and lost to daily on all three; see `research/FINDINGS.md`.
- Swing-low trail beat random entries with the same exit at the 98th percentile or better on all three. At a 20% stake: IAU +1.1%, PPLT +1.4%, BTC +12.3% a year on the pot (about +15% combined, ~80% of it BTC), max drawdowns -4%, -9%, -16%. At 33%, scale roughly by 1.65. Details in `research/FINDINGS.md`.
- Nulls on IAU train: volume, volatility, lower-high clear, fair value gaps add nothing as entry filters. The fresh 9/21 cross helped gold in training but did not hold under the fixed/ATR exits in 2015-2026.
